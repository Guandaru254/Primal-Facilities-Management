OUR SERVICES

Our Efficient Solutions

We offer a full range of facility management services designed to keep your property running smoothly.

HVAC Maintenance & Repair

Servicing and maintaining air conditioning, ventilation, and refrigeration systems for optimal efficiency.

Electrical & Plumbing Services

Expert installation, repair, and maintenance of electrical and plumbing systems.

Kitchen & Laundry Appliance Services

Professional maintenance and repair of residential and commercial kitchen and laundry appliances, including ovens, dishwashers, refrigerators, washing machines, and cooking ranges.

Cleaning & Janitorial Services

High-quality cleaning services tailored to homes, offices, and commercial spaces.

Pest Control Services

Eliminating pests through safe, eco-friendly methods to maintain a healthy living and working environment.

Swimming Pool Servicing

Regular cleaning, chemical balancing, and equipment maintenance to keep pools in perfect condition.

CCTV & Internet Installation

Installing, configuring, and maintaining security surveillance systems and high-speed internet solutions.

Security & Access Control

Providing secure environments with modern surveillance systems, access control, and remote security servicing for enhanced protection.

Upholstery Restoration & Cleaning

Expert restoration and deep cleaning of sofas, carpets, and furniture to extend their lifespan.

Fire Safety & Compliance

Ensuring compliance with fire safety regulations through preventive maintenance and inspections.

Moving and Transport Services

We offer a professional, secure, and efficient moving & transport Service tailored to meet the needs of businesses and institutions of all sizes.

Carpet Cleaning Services

We ensure your carpets are cleaned to the highest standards, without causing damage to the fabric.

Exhauster Services

Our exhauster services involve the use of specialized vacuum tankers (also called exhauster trucks) to pump out and transport waste from sanitation systems to approved disposal sites.

Borehole Services

We provide professional borehole drilling services to ensure access to quality underground water.

House Renovation Services

We assess the structure of your home and carry out any necessary remodeling or reinforcement to improve functionality, safety, and design
